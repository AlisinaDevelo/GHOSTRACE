# Code of Conduct

GHOSTRACE follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

Expected behavior includes:

- using considerate and inclusive language;
- accepting good-faith technical disagreement;
- focusing criticism on ideas and evidence;
- respecting privacy and never requesting real journal data in public.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal
attacks, doxxing, publication of private information, sexualized conduct, and
deliberate attempts to bypass the project's privacy or safety boundaries.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do
not include journal contents or other sensitive material. Maintainers will review
reports fairly, protect confidentiality where possible, and may remove content or
restrict participation when necessary.
