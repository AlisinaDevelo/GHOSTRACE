## Goal
Package the research questions, protocols, synthetic corpus, analysis, results, limitations, and exact code revisions for independent review and academic use.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tables and figures trace to machine-readable inputs and scripts, with checksums and licenses.
- [ ] The paper or report separates implemented capabilities, measured results, planned work, negative findings, and external-validity limits.
