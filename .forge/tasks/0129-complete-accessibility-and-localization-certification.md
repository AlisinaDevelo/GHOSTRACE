## Goal
Verify that installation, consent, gaps, explanations, policy, export, deletion, repair, and incident states remain usable with assistive technology and translated text.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] VoiceOver, keyboard-only, contrast, zoom, reduced-motion, dynamic text, focus, and error-recovery task scripts pass.
- [ ] Pseudo-localization and representative translations preserve evidence level, negation, uncertainty, dates, numbers, and identifiers.
- [ ] Accessibility regressions are required release checks with documented exception and expiry handling.

## Context
A privacy control that cannot be perceived or operated is not an effective control.

## Notes
Planned in the 2026–2031 GHOSTRACE program. Completion requires the acceptance evidence above; issue closure alone is not evidence.
