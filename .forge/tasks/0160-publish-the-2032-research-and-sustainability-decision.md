## Goal
Use five years of technical, research, community, maintenance, risk, and adoption evidence to choose the next program rather than assuming indefinite expansion.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The review reports delivered and abandoned goals, maintenance cost, issue flow, contributor health, platform risk, research findings, user value, and privacy incidents.
- [ ] Options include focused maintenance, a funded v2 program, platform expansion, research-only status, transfer, or orderly archival with support obligations.
- [ ] The decision publishes 2032 objectives, non-goals, resources, owners, evidence gaps, and stop conditions without overstating adoption or impact.

## Context
A durable project needs an explicit continuation or retirement decision backed by evidence.

## Notes
Planned in the 2026–2031 GHOSTRACE program. Completion requires the acceptance evidence above; issue closure alone is not evidence.
